Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Clipping Factory ("the app") is a desktop application made by Stream.Latin, an independent creator project ("we", "us"). It helps creators turn livestreams and videos they own, or have permission to use, into short vertical clips, and post them to TikTok when the user chooses to. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

The short version

  • The app runs on your own Windows computer. We do not run servers that receive your data.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your information, and we do not show ads or use tracking or analytics.
  • Nothing is sent to TikTok unless you approve that exact video, and it arrives as a draft: you decide in the TikTok app whether and how to post it.

Information from TikTok

When you connect a TikTok account with "Log in with TikTok", you choose which permissions to grant. The app requests:

  • user.info.basic: your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar, used to show which account is connected.
  • video.upload: used only to send a video you approved to your TikTok inbox as a draft. You finish and publish it yourself in the TikTok app; Clipping Factory does not publish to your account.

The app checks the processing status of the drafts it sends so it can show you whether they arrived.

TikTok gives the app access tokens so it can act on your behalf. These tokens are stored only on your computer, in Windows Credential Manager. They are not stored in the app's database, not written to logs, and never sent anywhere except to TikTok.

Videos and other content

The videos you record, import or edit, their transcripts and your settings are stored on your computer. A video is sent to TikTok only when you post it. If you turn on the optional AI features, the transcript text of a clip (not your TikTok data) is sent to Anthropic's API to suggest titles or memes; this is off by default.

How long information is kept

Information stays on your computer until you delete it. Disconnecting a TikTok account in the app asks TikTok to revoke the app's access and deletes the stored tokens. You can also remove the app's access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions.

California residents

We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising. You can ask what information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing the address below.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age to use TikTok where you live).
