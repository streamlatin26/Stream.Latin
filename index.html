Clipping Factory

A desktop app that turns livestreams and videos you own, or have permission to use, into short vertical clips with captions, and sends the ones you approve to your TikTok drafts.

What it does

  • Records live channels and imports videos you have permission to clip.
  • Finds the best moments and makes 9:16 clips with captions in English and Spanish.
  • Lets you review and edit every clip: start/end, framing, caption style, overlays.
  • Sends clips you approve to your TikTok inbox as drafts with "Log in with TikTok". You finish and post them yourself in the TikTok app; Clipping Factory never publishes to your account.

Your data stays on your computer

The app runs on Windows. TikTok access tokens are stored in Windows Credential Manager on your PC. We do not run servers that receive your data, and we never sell it. Details: Privacy Policy.
